About Us

Internova is an Internship & Placement Hub designed to connect students, colleges, faculty mentors, and companies on a single platform.

Our platform simplifies the complete internship lifecycle by providing tools for internship management, attendance tracking, task management, student progress monitoring, performance evaluation, and certificate management.

Internova aims to create a transparent and efficient ecosystem where students can build their professional skills, mentors can guide and evaluate students, colleges can manage placement activities, and companies can collaborate with talented students.
